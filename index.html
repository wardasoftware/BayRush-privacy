Privacy Policy

BayRush Bus Jam Puzzle

Effective date: November 19, 2026

WardaSoftware ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use BayRush Bus Jam Puzzle ("BayRush" or the "Game"). It covers information stored on your device and information processed by third-party services used to support gameplay, analytics, advertising, crash diagnostics, purchases, and optional game services.

Important: BayRush's advertising consent choices and its analytics/crash reporting are separate. Google UMP is used for advertising privacy choices where applicable; an advertising choice does not by itself turn off Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, or crash diagnostics.

1. Information handled by BayRush

Gameplay data stored on your device

The Game stores gameplay and preference information locally, which may include your current level, completed levels, coins, boosters, unlocked content, selected bus, sound preferences, and other game settings. This information is used to run the Game and preserve progress on the device. It may be lost if you clear the Game's storage, uninstall it, or use an in-game reset option.

BayRush does not operate a custom game server for this local gameplay information. Some supported progress may be synchronized through Google Play Games if you sign in and that feature is available in your version of the Game.

Analytics — Firebase Analytics and Mixpanel

BayRush uses Firebase Analytics and Mixpanel to understand how the Game is used and to improve stability and gameplay. Depending on the current SDK configuration and features used, analytics events may include level starts and completions, attempts, progress, booster use, ad lifecycle events, purchase status, app version, platform, timestamps, and pseudonymous app or device identifiers.

We do not ask you to submit your name or email address to use the core puzzle game. Do not include personal information in any optional message or support request unless it is needed to respond to you.

Crash and technical diagnostics — Firebase Crashlytics

Crashlytics may process crash reports, error details, stack traces, app state, device model, operating system and app version, timestamps, and diagnostic identifiers. We use this information to find and fix technical problems.

Advertising — Google AdMob

BayRush may show interstitial and rewarded advertisements. Google AdMob and its advertising partners may process information such as your IP address, device and app information, advertising identifiers where available and permitted, ad requests, impressions, clicks, and fraud-prevention signals. Depending on your location, privacy choices, and applicable requirements, advertising may be personalized or non-personalized.

BayRush uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to present or manage advertising privacy choices where required. You can use the Game's Ad privacy option when it is available, and you can also manage advertising controls in your device settings. UMP choices relate to advertising processing; they do not necessarily control analytics or crash reporting.

Purchases — Google Play

If you make an in-app purchase, Google Play processes the payment. BayRush does not receive or store your full payment-card or bank-account credentials. Google Play and the Game may process product details, transaction identifiers, and purchase status to complete the transaction and provide the purchased item or entitlement.

Google Play Games

If you choose to sign in to Google Play Games, Google may process your gaming profile and supported game progress for sign-in and synchronization features available in BayRush. These services are governed by Google's policies and the settings on your Google account.

2. How information is used

Information described in this Policy may be used to:

  • operate the puzzle game and save local progress and preferences;
  • measure gameplay and improve levels, features, and usability;
  • detect crashes, diagnose errors, and improve app stability;
  • deliver and measure ads, apply privacy choices, and help prevent invalid ad activity;
  • process purchases and provide purchased content; and
  • provide optional Google Play Games features when you choose to use them.

3. When information is shared

We share information with service providers only as needed for the functions described in this Policy. Depending on the feature and configuration, these providers include:

  • Google AdMob and UMP for advertising and advertising privacy choices;
  • Firebase Analytics for usage measurement;
  • Firebase Crashlytics for crash diagnostics;
  • Mixpanel for product analytics; and
  • Google Play Billing and Google Play Games for purchases and supported game services.

These providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies. We do not sell personal information in exchange for money. Some privacy laws use broader definitions of "sale" or "sharing" that may include certain advertising-related disclosures; where required, applicable choices are provided through the relevant consent or privacy controls.

4. Retention and deletion

Local gameplay data remains on your device until it is cleared, reset, or removed by uninstalling the Game. Deleting local data does not delete information already held by Google, Firebase, Mixpanel, Google Play, or Google Play Games. Those providers apply their own retention and deletion practices.

You can contact us to ask about information associated with BayRush. We will review requests and respond as required by applicable law and based on the information and controls available to us. Purchase records may be retained by Google Play for transaction, legal, security, or accounting purposes. You can also review Google account and Play Games settings for controls over supported synchronized game data.

5. Your privacy choices and rights

Your rights depend on where you live and may include rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to certain processing. You may:

  • use BayRush's Ad privacy option, when available, to revisit advertising choices;
  • use your Android settings to manage or reset the advertising identifier and notification permissions;
  • clear local Game data or uninstall BayRush to remove locally stored progress; and
  • contact us with a privacy request using the details below.

Google UMP advertising consent does not currently provide a general analytics opt-out in BayRush. Contact us if you have a request about analytics or diagnostics, and we will review it under applicable law and the controls available from the relevant service.

6. Children

BayRush is not designed to request a player's name, email address, or other direct contact information for core gameplay. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can review the request. If the Game is directed to children or we learn that a child is using it in a way that requires additional protections, we will take steps required by applicable law.

7. International processing and security

Our service providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. They describe their international processing and safeguards in their own policies. We use reasonable measures appropriate to our role to protect information, but no method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.
